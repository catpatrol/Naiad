# SS Cascade v11 — FIELD MANUAL
### Read the chart, size the trade, take the exit · for v11.0.2+ · one purpose: maximum expectancy
*(Standalone quick reference. The Execution Playbook v1.1 carries the full doctrine.)*

---

## 1. The five-second read (always first)

| Cue | Tells you |
|---|---|
| **Background tint** | Campaign direction (green long / red short) and **tier**: deep shade = full campaign (structure aligned) · faint shade = provisional (half size, B-cap) |
| **Bands** | Where a trade can exist: amber **Z1** (gov 9) · purple **Z2** (gov 89) · cyan **Z3** (89–200 band, aligned campaigns only). **No band under price = no trade available** |
| **Stop step-line** | Green below / red above = live campaign with entries; its level is your invalidation |

Machine states: tint flip = armed (arrow may be hidden by the whipsaw filter — the
tint is the truth) → price enters a band = the only moment needing your eyes →
grade circle = entry → dots/diamonds = adds → TPW/X = defense/death.

## 2. Glyphs at a glance

**Lines:** yellow = exec 9 (trigger line) · purple = exec 89 · thin blue = exec 200.

| Glyph | Meaning | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Solid ▲/▼ | Full campaign armed | Nothing — wait for a band tag |
| Faded ▲/▼ | **Provisional** campaign armed | Wait; pre-commit half size, Z1/Z2 only |
| "89x200" dot | Live campaign upgrades to full | Z3 + A/A+ now possible; normalize size on new signals |
| **A+ circle** | R1 entry: aligned campaign, tag inside sniper pocket (0.5–0.786) ∩ zone | **Full size.** Best trade the system produces |
| **A circle** | R1: aligned campaign + sub-governor TF agrees | Full / near-full size |
| **B circle** | R1: provisional campaign, or no sub-TF agreement | **Half size** |
| "C" label | Late entry (missed R1), zone+structure gated | Smallest size or skip; journal it |
| Tiny circle | R2+ add signal | Add ≤ initial unit; stop ratchets |
| Tiny diamond | CONFIRM (9/89 re-cross) | Add if positioned; not an initiation |
| "V" arrow | Capitulation reversal (rare, gate-exempt) | Trade it like a B until upgraded |
| **TPW flag** | ≥2 timeframes flipped against you | Bank 25–50%, tighten, stop adding |
| **X** | Campaign dead (band lost 3 closes) | **Flat on the close. Always** |
| Tiny TF triangles | 5m/1H/4H/12H telemetry | Context only — never entries |

## 3. The entry (the only one worth taking)

The shape: campaign armed → price pulls back into a band (the 5m slips under its
yellow 9 — that *is* the pullback) → **inside the band, a 5m close reclaims the 9
and the next close confirms** → the grade circle prints → you fill near that close,
stop at the printed line (tag extreme ∓ 0.5 ATR). Right entries take heat measured
in a handful of 5m bars.

Sizing = grade, decided before the circle prints: A+ full · A full/near-full ·
B half · C token/skip. Zone quality at equal grade: **Z3 > Z2 > Z1** to start a
campaign; Z1 is for adds.

**60-second check:** tint direction + depth → armed? → which band (Z2/Z3 preferred)
→ circle on a *confirming* close? → stop distance in R → opposing MTF triangles? →
fill, stop, done. **Never chase a runner — the next band tag is the trade.**

## 4. Managing the campaign

Every later band-tag PRIME (tiny circle) is an add; the stop line only ever
tightens, so total risk stays flat or shrinks while size grows. Add only while
earlier tranches sit at/beyond breakeven after the ratchet. An 89x200 dot
mid-campaign lifts the throttle: deeper adds, better grades, full units from there.

## 5. The exit ladder (in order)

1. **TPW** → partials + tighten. One TPW a strong trend can absorb; two in the same
   leg, get small.
2. **Yellow-line trail** — trend extended, then a 5m close back through the 9 with
   no quick reclaim → trail out / take another partial (no glyph; read the line).
3. **X or an opposite solid arrow** → flat, immediately, entirely. No averaging,
   no re-entry until a fresh arming.
Extension bonus: price ≥ ~2 ATR beyond the yellow 9 = stretched — bank something.
The stop line is the disaster floor, not the exit plan.

## 6. Never

At the arrow · outside a band · full size on a faded arrow · against the tint ·
wider than the printed stop · after an X · into a "C" you haven't location-checked.

## 7. Maximum-expectancy summary

Wait for the circle **in the band, in the tint, at the tier's size** — most of the
edge is the waiting. Concentrate risk where the study paid: A/A+ initiations in
Z2/Z3, pyramided with ratcheting risk, exited by the ladder instead of by feelings.
Half-size everything provisional, and let the budgeted small B losses buy your seat
in the campaigns that pay for the year.
