# 1. @tradermatt (Matt) — Pure Price-Action Supply/Demand Practitioner

### Core Identity & What Makes Him Different
11+ years of pure price-action trading on forex and Bitcoin. Explicitly rejects indicators, EMAs, volume profile, order flow, and oscillators. The edge is distilled to: mark structural extremes → wait for price to range at them → trade the breakout in the direction of higher-timeframe structure with a tight stop. Extremely process-oriented and psychology-aware. The most “minimalist” of the four.

### 1. How He Reads and Reacts to Areas of Interest
- **AOI = Supply & Demand zones** (clusters of price action at the extremes of prior structure) and the ranges that form at those zones.  
- “Price Action Zones” (PAZ — the noisy space between structures) are deliberately avoided; they are random.  
- Price is observed to gravitate to the extremes of S/D.  
- Multi-timeframe drilling is used to locate the true defining level (a weekly may appear broken while the H4 level still holds).  
- Round numbers frequently act as significant S/R flips.  
- Mid-range of a larger range is checked for respect; a genuine range shows structure at highs, lows, *and* mid.

**Reaction sequence (repeated across years):**
1. Mark key S/D on the relevant TF.
2. Wait for price to arrive and *range* at the zone (often LTF).
3. Prefer a fake-out in the opposite direction first.
4. Enter on the break of the range that creates a new structural extreme (lower low or higher high) with strength, in line with HTF bias.
5. Stop placed beyond the extreme of the range/high/low.

Example language (July 2026): “mark key level → wait for price to range at key level → price fakes out to the upside → price drops through the range with strength to create a lower low → enter with stop above high.”

### 2. Market Structure, Accumulation/Distribution, Range Breakout
- Higher highs / higher lows = permission to hold directional bias.  
- Market structure break is the actual signal to flip bias — not before.  
- After an impulse, a range forms at the extreme (balance / absorption). Compression resolves either by impulse out or by a liquidity grab first.  
- The range-breakout itself is the primary trade. Ranges that respect both extremes *and* the mid are considered higher quality.  
- Trend-following is described as “fewer, bigger ones” — not more trades simply because price is moving hard.  
- Historical evolution: previously faded large moves into key areas with limit orders (high RR, low strike rate, psychologically costly). Switched to waiting for the range to form and then trading the breakout for better expectancy and emotional sustainability. This change is visible in commentary from 2025–2026.

### 3. Subsystem Used to Trade the Range
**Primary subsystem = LTF range formation at HTF S/D extreme + directional breakout confirmation.**  
- HTF structure supplies the bias.  
- LTF supplies the precise range and the breakout trigger (often requiring a clean structural break + strength).  
- No secondary indicators; the confirmation is pure PA (fake-out + break + strength).  
- Mid-range respect acts as a quality filter on the larger range.

### 4. Adjusting Sizing to Confluence-Dependent Triggers
- Sizing is treated as the real determinant of outcomes: “you can be right on direction and still lose money… position sizing decides which one happens more.”  
- When the urge to re-enter is strong, take *one small* position only — otherwise multiple small ones accumulate into oversized risk.  
- Prop (funded) capital and personal capital are managed with different risk postures.  
- Warning signs of incorrect sizing are catalogued (needing every trade to work, relief instead of satisfaction, balance-checking more than setup-checking, mood changed by a single loss, revenge sizing).  
- Higher confluence (clean S/D + well-defined range + mid respect + clear structure bias + strength on break) supports fuller size; low-confluence or “itch” trades are deliberately undersized.

### Trading Logic Extracted from Charts & Updates
- **Watchlist candidate:** Clear S/D zone at a structural extreme that has already shown reaction, preferably with multi-TF confluence and round-number alignment.  
- **Entry trigger:** Price ranges at the zone → fake-out → break of the range that creates a new structural extreme (LL or HH) with momentum.  
- **Management:** Stop beyond the range extreme. Targets often prior opposing structure or measured moves. Adds only after confirmation (e.g., post-news). Patience is repeatedly emphasised; many days are spent waiting.  
- **Profitability drivers:** Location edge (S/D extremes) + tight risk on confirmed breaks + psychological discipline that prevents over-trading and revenge. Asymmetry comes from letting winners run while cutting losers quickly at the structural invalidation.  
- **Most important principles:**  
  1. Process over prediction.  
  2. Wait for the market to declare intent via range behaviour.  
  3. Structure break > opinion.  
  4. Sizing and emotional control are part of the edge.  
  5. Simplicity is a feature, not a limitation.

# 2. @Trader_XO (XO) — Structure + Value/Profile Rotational Trader with Selective Orderflow

### Core Identity & What Makes Him Different
Long-term discretionary trader (9+ years referenced) with a large following and mentorship content. Price action and market structure are primary; volume profiles, VWAPs (weekly, monthly, anchored), POCs, and selective orderflow/rhythm are secondary confluence tools used mainly for execution timing and profit-taking. Explicit process culture around expectancy, journaling (pre-, during-, and post-trade), and scaling only after proof. Win rate often cited in the 30–35 % range because first entries are aggressive; second attempts are higher quality once asymmetry clarifies.

### 1. How He Reads and Reacts to Areas of Interest
- **AOI = High-quality structural levels + value-area edges + prior/developing VWAPs + POCs + failed-auction zones.**  
- “The only area where I’m interested in contemplating potential longs” is frequently a rounded retest of key lows or a failed auction below value that shows absorption.  
- Multi-TF structure (weekly/daily 12/25 EMAs often used as dynamic filters).  
- Acceptance vs rejection is the core language: does price *hold and trade* beyond a level, or does it fail and return?  
- Orderflow/rhythm at inflection points elevates pure structure reading: “study and observe the rhythm and orderflow of price at those key inflection points… Combine the two.”

**Reaction protocol:**
- Sell upper extremes / buy lower extremes of established ranges until *acceptance* outside the range is proven.  
- Failed auction below a value-area low → constructive for longs (absorption of selling).  
- “Chipping into” positions rather than all-in.  
- Counter-trend ideas are taken only with strong confluence and still treated cautiously against HTF EMAs.

### 2. Market Structure, Accumulation/Distribution, Range Breakout
- Market structure shifts + cascading HTF MAs signal potential reversals. In trends, “let the trends play out.”  
- Ranges are treated as rotational value areas. The 2024 BTC value-area range was traded rotationally for many months with the explicit plan: “Sell the upper extremes. Buy the lower extremes… Until the market finds acceptance outside of that range.”  
- Distribution is suspected when price trades back through key VWAPs with acceptance.  
- Breakouts are not chased; acceptance (price trading and holding outside prior value) is required. Expansion is confirmed only after failed attempts at the extremes are resolved.  
- Historical progression: heavy rotational focus on the major 2024 range; later refinement toward HTF trend channels + EMAs for longer-term spot accumulation while keeping the same execution toolkit for shorter-term rotations.

### 3. Subsystem Used to Trade the Range
**Primary subsystem = Value-area / Profile rotational trading with acceptance filter.**  
- Define the range via volume profile (prior year, composite, or significant period).  
- Trade mean-reversion between value extremes; POC is frequently the first objective.  
- Developing and anchored VWAPs act as dynamic references.  
- Confirmation via acceptance/rejection; orderflow/delta secondary for execution and profit-taking.  
- Failed auctions at the extremes function as high-probability reversal locations (spring-like behaviour).

### 4. Adjusting Sizing to Confluence-Dependent Triggers
- Formal process framework includes Risk Management, Position Sizing / Kelly Criterion, and expectancy calculation.  
- “I can’t scale up randomness.” Size is reduced when testing or when edge is not yet confirmed on sufficient sample.  
- First entry is often aggressive (contributing to ~30–35 % win rate); subsequent adds or second attempts are higher quality once the trade has developed and asymmetry is clearer.  
- Journaling is continuous (pre-trade plan, during lifecycle, post-trade review of both mental and technical performance).  
- Higher confluence (value edge + VWAP + failed auction + structure alignment) supports fuller size; lower-confluence or counter-trend ideas are smaller or avoided.

### Trading Logic Extracted from Charts & Updates
- **Watchlist candidate:** Value-area extreme, prior VWAP, POC, or failed-auction zone that aligns with HTF structure; preferably with stacked supply/demand above/below.  
- **Entry trigger:** Acceptance/rejection evidence or failed auction + absorption at the level; often “chipping in” rather than single large order.  
- **Management:** First target frequently the POC or opposing value edge. Partial profits taken; runners can be held for larger rotational or trend moves. Stops beyond the structural invalidation (acceptance the wrong way).  
- **Profitability drivers:** Asymmetry from trading the extremes of established value ranges + disciplined acceptance filter that keeps him out of low-quality mid-range chop + process that forces journaling and expectancy awareness. Low win rate is offset by favourable risk-reward on the higher-quality second attempts and by longer-term spot accumulation at asymmetric levels.  
- **Most important principles:**  
  1. Price action and structure are primary; everything else is secondary confluence.  
  2. Acceptance/rejection is the truth filter.  
  3. Trade the range rotationally until the market proves otherwise.  
  4. Journal continuously and scale only proven edge.  
  5. First entries can be aggressive; second entries should be higher quality.

# 3. @CrypNuevo — Liquidity & Market-Psychology Range Projector

### Core Identity & What Makes Him Different
“Not a bull, not a bear. Just TA. (Liquidity & market psychology).” Focus is on mapping liquidity pools (large bodies, equal highs/lows, long-wick imbalances), psychological levels, and EMA acceptance, then projecting multi-week to multi-month range behaviour. Highly visual and projection-oriented; custom TradingView indicator packages weeks, dual liquidity-pool formulas, sessions, and multi-TF 50EMA. Consistency of language and method across 2024–2026 is striking.

### 1. How He Reads and Reacts to Areas of Interest
- **AOI = Liquidity pools (LPs), long-wick zones (imbalances / OI gaps that “should” fill), mid-range levels, key EMAs (especially 1D and 4H 50EMA), and psychological round numbers.**  
- Liquidity is repeatedly described as a magnet. Accumulated liquidations (long or short) are treated as fuel the market will rebalance.  
- LTF consolidations between EMAs are read as “liquidity build-up” preceding the next impulse.  
- Momentum shifts and liquidity trendlines appear as early-warning tools.

**Reaction protocol:**
- Inside a defined range: target mid-range or next LP; take profits early in low-quality / “boring” conditions.  
- At extremes or long wicks: look for deviation + reclaim or 100 % wick fill.  
- Acceptance above mid-range or 1D50EMA → continuation toward higher LPs.  
- Loss of key EMA or range low → bias change or deeper target.  
- Explicit caution against catching knives when “thin air” exists below a level.

### 2. Market Structure, Accumulation/Distribution, Range Breakout
- HTF range or uptrend context frames everything.  
- LTF sideways between EMAs = liquidity accumulation.  
- Long-wick zones interpreted as trapped inventory.  
- Rising channels are recognised but treated as finite (“these patterns aren’t infinite and end up breaking”).  
- Breakouts are traded as continuation once acceptance is confirmed (e.g., above 1D50EMA then mid-range). Invalidation is clear: acceptance below the range low or key wick zone flips bias. Prefer controlled moves that hold EMAs.  
- Multi-month drawings are revisited and updated; projections made weeks or months earlier are referenced when price arrives.

### 3. Subsystem Used to Trade the Range
**Primary subsystem = HTF range geometry + Liquidity Pool map + EMA acceptance filter.**  
- Define bounding range and mid.  
- Map LPs and wick-fill targets.  
- Use LTF EMA consolidation as the build-up signal.  
- Execution trigger = acceptance (holding above/below key EMA or mid) or reaction from LP/wick.  
- In low-quality ranges the subsystem defaults to early profit-taking rather than holding for full targets.  
- Custom indicator keeps the liquidity and week maps visible so the operator does not forget key levels.

### 4. Adjusting Sizing to Confluence-Dependent Triggers
- Explicit risk language: “Looks a bit fragile so not a good place to risk much capital.”  
- High-risk knife-catching zones or thin-air areas are undersized or avoided.  
- Confluence (HTF range + LP + EMA acceptance + momentum confirmation) allows normal size.  
- Market conditions (volume, CEX interest, overall participation) affect opportunity quality; activity and size scale with conditions rather than forced activity.  
- Early profit-taking is recommended in “boring” ranges.

### Trading Logic Extracted from Charts & Updates
- **Watchlist candidate:** Clear HTF range with defined mid, nearby LPs or long-wick zones, and key EMAs that can act as acceptance filters.  
- **Entry trigger:** Acceptance above/below the critical EMA or mid-range, or reaction from a mapped LP / wick-fill zone after liquidity has been taken.  
- **Management:** Targets are next LP or mid-range; early partials in low-quality conditions. Stops beyond the invalidation (acceptance the wrong way or loss of the key EMA). Projections are managed rather than rigidly held.  
- **Profitability drivers:** Accurate mapping of where liquidity sits (magnet effect) + disciplined acceptance filter that keeps him from fighting the prevailing structure + multi-timeframe projection that provides context so he is not surprised by larger moves. Consistency of method across years compounds the edge.  
- **Most important principles:**  
  1. Liquidity is a magnet — map it.  
  2. Acceptance is the confirmation, not the first touch.  
  3. Ranges are the dominant regime; treat channels as temporary.  
  4. Reduce risk when the map shows thin air or fragility.  
  5. Projections provide context; LTF acceptance provides the trigger.

# 4. @Stefan_B_Trades (Stefan) — Fibonacci + Range + Liquidity Hunter (Location-First)

### Core Identity & What Makes Him Different
Account active from early 2024. Bio centres on Fibonacci, Range, PA and Liquidity hunting. Strong emphasis on overall location (premium / equilibrium / discount) over local structure. Treats ~80 % of price action as ranging and therefore prefers fading breakouts; genuine breakout setups are reserved for deep-discount locations that also carry Fib confluence. Clear invalidations, pre-framed multi-setup plans, and rapid movement of positions to risk-free status. Live sessions and subscription content.

### 1. How He Reads and Reacts to Areas of Interest
- **AOI / POI = Points of Interest that combine Fibonacci levels (.618 / .786 especially), horizontal pivots, equal highs/lows (eqls), liquidity raids (previous weekly low, etc.), and trendline confluence.**  
- Always starts with overall location and context relative to the larger range or swing. Local structure alone produces “tunnel vision” and confirmation bias.  
- “Deep premium → only look for shorts. Equilibrium → take partials / pay the trader. Deep discount → only look for longs.”  
- Liquidity raids of key levels create the highest-probability reaction zones.

### 2. Market Structure, Accumulation/Distribution, Range Breakout
- Structure (LH/LL or trendline breaks) is secondary to location.  
- Ranges are the dominant regime. Default stance is to *fade* breakouts because most are false.  
- True breakout setups are reserved for deep-discount locations, preferably into .618/.786 Fib confluence with trendlines.  
- Resulting behaviour: systematically short greed/hopium and long fear/desperation.  
- Liquidity hunts clear stops and create fuel.  
- Historical progression: early posts already show Fib + liquidity + range language; later posts emphasise higher-timeframe focus (4H+), pre-framed setups with invalidations, and reduced LTF noise. System appears stable and increasingly selective.

### 3. Subsystem Used to Trade the Range
**Primary subsystem = Location-filtered Fib + Liquidity POI trading inside ranges.**  
- Measure the range/move with Fib (or Gann).  
- Identify POIs at Fib + horizontal + liquidity confluence.  
- Default: fade extremes (long range lows / short range highs).  
- Exception: breakout only when price is in deep discount (for longs) with additional confluence.  
- Every setup carries a clear invalidation; risk only what can be afforded if the invalidation hits.  
- Multi-setup framing (often several planned trades on MTF/HTF) with both long and short ideas prepared in advance.

### 4. Adjusting Sizing to Confluence-Dependent Triggers
- Risk is defined by the invalidation distance and personal affordability (“I only risked what I could’ve afford”).  
- Positions are moved to risk-free as quickly as possible (TP1 hit → stop to break-even or better).  
- High-confluence POIs (Fib + liquidity raid + correct location + trendline) support normal or full planned size.  
- Lower-confluence or local-structure-only ideas are avoided or heavily reduced.  
- On Fridays / before key closes, new risk is often reduced or paused until weekly/monthly structure is clear.  
- Emphasis on patience, plan adherence, and emotional control as the “actual system” alongside risk management.

### Trading Logic Extracted from Charts & Updates
- **Watchlist candidate:** Fib-measured POI that sits in the correct location (premium for shorts, discount for longs), preferably with liquidity (eqls, prior weekly levels) and trendline confluence.  
- **Entry trigger:** Reaction at the POI (rejection, liquidity raid + reclaim, or structure confirmation in the expected direction).  
- **Management:** Clear invalidation level. Rapid movement to risk-free once TP1 is hit. Multiple partials; runners can be held if the larger location thesis remains intact. New risk is often paused near major candle closes.  
- **Profitability drivers:** Location filter prevents the common error of longing into premium or shorting into discount. Fib measurement provides objective targets and invalidations. Fast risk-free status reduces emotional pressure and allows the trader to stay in the higher-probability direction. Pre-framed plans reduce discretionary error.  
- **Most important principles:**  
  1. Location (premium/discount) comes first; local structure is secondary.  
  2. Most of the time the market is ranging — fade extremes.  
  3. Breakouts require deep-discount confluence.  
  4. Every setup has a clear invalidation; only risk what you can afford.  
  5. Move to risk-free quickly; do not babysit positions.

### Shared Themes Relevant to Level Selection & Range Mechanics
- All four prioritise **location** (extremes, value, liquidity concentrations, measured discount/premium) over pure timing confluence.  
- Ranges are treated as the dominant regime or as balance phases; breakouts or failed breakouts are the actionable events.  
- Liquidity concepts (stop-runs, trapped inventory, magnets) appear in every system in different language.  
- Acceptance / rejection / mid-range respect / structural confirmation are recurring decision criteria.  
- Sizing is universally a function of confluence quality and personal risk process, never fixed.  
- Process discipline, patience, and emotional control are treated as integral parts of the edge, not soft add-ons.

### Practical Implications for a Level-Selection System
1. Favour levels that are *tested or multi-confluent* (prior touches that held, mid-range respect, Fib + horizontal + liquidity, value-edge + VWAP).  
2. Require a secondary confirmation layer before acting on a range extreme or breakout (LTF range break, acceptance, deep-discount filter, or structural extreme).  
3. Explicit invalidation and rapid movement to risk-free status improve longevity.  
4. The highest-conviction reactions occur at extremes that have already shown structural defence or liquidity concentration.  
5. Projection / context layers (multi-month range maps, HTF value areas) keep the trader oriented so LTF noise does not generate false urgency.
