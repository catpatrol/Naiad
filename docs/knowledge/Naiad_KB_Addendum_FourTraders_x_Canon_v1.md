## 4 · Applications register — continuation (B10–B18)

**B10 · Range-quality grade = mid-respect count** *(annex column)*. matt's criterion — a genuine range shows structure at highs, lows, *and* mid — becomes a counted grade on every detected range; the census then answers whether mid-respected ranges fade and break differently. One column, no rule surface.

**B11 [VETO] · Named composite pattern for the miner's confirmatory list.** matt's full sequence — *LTF range forms at an HTF wall → fake-out (spring) → break creating a new structural extreme, with strength* — pre-registered as a named macro-pattern beside the ≥4h-within-3-rungs motif. It is expressible in Q1c's alphabet and it is the single most repeated practitioner setup in the document. One word ("composite") includes it.

**B12 · Convergence note, no action.** XO's aggressive-first / higher-quality-second doctrine is the practitioner statement of exactly what E3 (LPS split) and TC-2 are measuring. Cite in TC-2's design brief.

**B13 · Wall attributes: equal-highs/lows and round numbers** *(annex stamps)*. "Eqls" = two-plus pivots within a tight ε at the same price — computable from existing pivot machinery as a liquidity attribute; round-number proximity as a second stamp. Counted, never weighted.

**B14 · Structure-density map ("thin air")** *(annex)*. CrypNuevo's caution — don't catch knives above thin air — becomes a wall-material density field; low-density gaps are context for targets and stops. Derivable from the same clusters; cheap.

**B15 · 50EMA — declined (lean).** CrypNuevo's 1D/4H 50EMA acceptance filter sits bracketed by our 25 and 89. Adding a sixth EMA widens the anti-fishing surface for a lens the fan already spans. Noted; not adopted unless the operator overrides.

**B16 · The 80%-ranging check** *(free print)*. The census's classifier work already computes time-in-BALANCE per lens; print it as a table against Stefan's folklore number. Costless calibration of the whole regime layer against practitioner intuition.

**B17 · The BE distinction, kept sharp.** Stefan's *partial-then-risk-free* is structurally the operator's registered ⅔-harvest/moonbag; the *naive* early BE-trail without harvest is the two-line architecture the study already falsified (−122 vs the band). 2b's harvest curves adjudicate the good version; the language stays disambiguated so the dead version never sneaks back in wearing the live one's name.

**B18 · SSv12 Pine design principle — projection persistence.** CrypNuevo's custom indicator exists partly "so the operator does not forget key levels." Registered for the Pine spec: the indicator remembers its mapped ranges, walls, and projections across sessions. The map does not forget.
