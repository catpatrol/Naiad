Transition detail

The chain is tight and adjacent while it is fast: 5m→15m runs 84%, 15m→30m 80%. It loosens at 30m→1H (63%), and then it breaks. From 1H, the cascade is more likely to fall back to 30m (48%) than to advance to 4H (29%). That stall is the structure the whole page is about.

The honest version · read this one

Does reaching a higher rung mean a bigger move?

Yes — but far less than the naive cut suggests, and the gap between those two answers is a lesson worth keeping. The outcome is measured over the 100 bars (8.3 hours) after the pullback. If you let the cascade keep counting for 48 hours, you are crediting crosses that fired after the outcome was already decided. Shrink the counting window and watch the effect deflate.

What the toggle shows

Why it matters. The inflated view shows a move jumping from 3.86 to 6.45 ATR when the cascade reaches 4H — a spectacular, untradeable number. The causal view, using only crosses that had already fired when you would have to decide, shows 3.94 → 4.46. Real, useful, a third the size. The difference is pure hindsight.

Sequence census

The most probable cascade orders

The first three rungs of every cascade that fired at least three, ranked by how often that exact order occurs. 83.6% of all cascades run strictly fast-to-slow — the ladder metaphor is not an idealisation, it is what the data does. Note the right-hand column: the common sequences are the shallow ones, and the rare ones carry the bigger moves.

Reading the tail

The top sequence, 5m→15m→30m, is 60.6% of all three-rung cascades and returns a middling 4.44 ATR. The sequences that reach a slow frame in three steps — 5m→15m→4H (2.3%, 6.41), 5m→15m→12H (1.9%, 6.69), 15m→30m→4H (1.1%, 7.22) — are individually rare and carry moves half again as large.

Same caution as above: these outcome figures use the full counting window, so they carry the same hindsight inflation. Treat the ordering as solid and the outcome column as indicative.

What this is not. Not a validated finding, not a trading rule, and not out-of-sample. Every figure is computed in-sample on exploration-classic data that has already been searched. The page exists to generate hypotheses precisely enough that they can be pre-registered and tested against the sealed lockbox — which is the only place any of it can earn the word "edge".
