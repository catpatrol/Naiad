## AMENDMENT A2 — THE ACCEPTANCE HOLD, NAMED. 2026-08-12 (ADDITIVE)

```
h = 2 bars  [VETO, operator-named 2026-08-12]
```

**What this constant is, and why it needed naming.** The v0.2 draft defined the acceptance test as
*"close beyond + hold h bars [VETO] vs deviation-reclaim"* (`CENSUS2A_CONTRACT_DRAFT_v0.2:135`).
The v0.2 -> v0.3 compression kept the noun phrases and **deleted that parenthetical**, so the
ratified body names an "acceptance head-to-head" without stating the rule that decides it, and `h`
appears nowhere in it. Run 3 halted CEN-6 rather than sweep `h`, because selecting a `[VETO]`
constant by search is what §N forbids.

`h` is now named. **CEN-6's acceptance rule is therefore, in full:**

> An excursion beyond a range boundary is ACCEPTED on a close-set member {1H, 4H, 12H} when the
> member's close is beyond the boundary and **holds beyond for h = 2 consecutive member bars**.
> It is DEVIATION-RECLAIMED when price closes back inside first. The two are the two branches of
> one episode, adjudicated by three clocks.

**Scope:** A2 names one previously-unvalued `[VETO]`. It re-pins nothing and alters no scored
definition. It unblocks CEN-6, and through it CEN-4's `verdict-open` and CEN-5b's `v0 band`.
