## 3 · MAIN FINDINGS (each [verified] in its run's build document)

**F1 · The exit anatomy is the whole game.** The resolved book is **12.3% winners carrying a mean
of +9.4R each** against a median loser of −1.04R. *Any exit study on this book is a study of tail
preservation* — the sentence that should hang over EngineV2's exit design.

**F2 · The incumbent loser decile was a position-size selector.** 97.5% one size, 38.6% one asset.
The A3-DECILE size-free per-asset ruler (validated by reconstructing realized_r from raw rows,
corr 0.993) is now the only honest loser cohort — and MC-1's dramatic grind figures inherited the
old bias. The true grind effect is ~7 points, direction-asymmetric, composite-ingredient material.

**F3 · The clock control (CEN-9) — humility, quantified.** EMA-anchored armings beat stratified
random instants by **+0.052 ATR** median terminal H100 — a real ordering that is **exactly
toll-sized** (0.026–0.059 ATR round-trip). And one non-EMA anchor — **the prior-extreme sweep from
below (+0.0845)** — edges out the armings (+0.0748). Descriptive, no intervals, registers nothing —
but it says: the EMA clock is not magic, location anchors compete, and the sweep (the trap's
footprint!) deserves a registered look. The operator's PO3 instinct just got independent,
outcome-side corroboration from the census's own control module.

**F4 · Registry levels rarely co-locate at armings** — 0.7% of instants carry ≥2 levels within
0.15×ATR. The *stacked-confluence* premise, as geometrically operationalized, is not how this tape
arranges itself at ignition. Confluence may still matter as *which single wall*, not *how many* —
an analysis-phase question, now askable on real data.

**F5 · RESPECTED is a breakout that failed to be born — measured.** On 1H, deviation-reclaims are
preceded by ~3× the price↔level refusal density of acceptances (0.226 vs 0.072/episode). The i-b
limb built in run 1 made the operator's range grammar quantitative.

**F6 · The verdict clock:** accept-rate is near-flat across {1H, 4H, 12H} (0.714→0.740) while
episode counts fall 2.7× — and on *fixed 48h windows* the **12H close is the least trap-prone**
(0.540 vs 1H's 0.803). The bar-count version reversed that order; units are destiny.

**F7 · Exit-alone ratcheting is bounded, twice.** 0/36 corners beat riding; the median campaign
never reclaims; the 4h/long-EMA/wide-cushion family carries positive *means* (+0.45R) on 0.19
beat-share — a few large saves. P-RAT-3 goes after exactly that shape with named statistics.

**F8 · The relay result stands, qualified.** P-REL-1b (+0.19 ATR) remains the census's one
survivor — with its witnesses' 0.65 correlation printed beside it forever. The Rotation Program is
therefore a **statistical necessity**: independent juries or permanent provisionality.

**F9 · Structure of armings:** COMPLETED 595 (+0.24 median H100) / ABORTED 252 (−0.48, mechanical)
/ ROTTED 1 — W_max=151 governs one arming in 848; the counter-arming is the real clock. COMPLETED's
IQR straddles zero: longer-lived ≠ better.

**F10 · The instrument hardened itself.** A fixture that tested a mock (F-PIN) was rewritten
against live code; a silent manifest deletion was repaired with MERGED_SECTIONS; the [VETO]
register closed over the contract; F-KEY made the thrice-appearing join-key defect a permanent
gate; two compressed-out definitions (witness-correlation, the sabotage parenthetical) were
restored and immediately mattered. The correction culture is the census's second product.

## 5 · THE ANALYSIS ARCHITECTURE — probing everything without poisoning anything

**The law that makes "as many angles as possible" safe:** *exploration is unlimited; belief is
rationed.* Two tiers, mechanically separated:

**TIER-E (EXPLORE — anything, everything, now).** Every probe runs on the cached parquets, prints
whatever it likes, and is stamped `EXPLORATION — ungated; promotion requires registration`. No
probe result may be cited as a finding; every probe is **logged** (question · tables touched ·
selection surface m · what looked interesting) in a probe ledger
(`exchange/reports/CENSUS2A_PROBE_LEDGER.md`, append-only). The guard doesn't forbid looking — it
prices remembering.

**TIER-P (PROMOTE — by ceremony only).** Anything interesting graduates by: registration with
named statistic + prior → scored on data not used to find it (held-out era slice, or next-cycle
replication, or the rotation universe) → FDR family declared → witness-correlation printed → TRG
or tail-exit-ratio beside it. The probe ledger's selection surface is the m that FDR charges.

**The probe matrix — the systematic enumeration.** Cross four axes; every cell is a legal Tier-E
probe:
- **OBJECTS** (12): armings · windows · triggers · seals · refusals i-a · refusals i-b · nested
  flags · FAN/KNOT states · verdict episodes · campaigns · orphan births · clock anchors.
- **RULERS** (6): terminal ATR-R at H20/H100/H500 · MFE · MAE · MFE/|MAE| · give-back ·
  time-to-event (hazard) — the last being the frame P-ARM-2 needs.
- **CONDITIONINGS** (10): direction · asset · era-half (both splits) · stamp subsets · composite
  grade · lens · fate class · trigger class · depth · registry-wall family at anchor.
- **ROBUSTNESS MOVES** (6): label-shuffle null · matched-instant null (CEN-9's machinery, reused) ·
  block bootstrap · LOAO · witness-correlation print · toll-line overlay.
12 × 6 × 10 × 6 ≈ **4,320 canonical probes** — enumerable, loggable, and mostly automatable as a
sweep the guard watches. Priority order (reviewer's lean, operator's to reorder): (P1) the
**sweep-from-below anchor** vs armings, properly intervaled — F3's provocation; (P2) the
**PO3-mirror arm (b)** counterfactual — the unexecuted exit; (P3) the **mean-carried ratchet
family** anatomy (which campaigns those +0.45R saves live in); (P4) **single-wall vs stacked
confluence** at armings — F4's question; (P5) the **orphan cohort** under the entry lens; (P6)
FAN-transition survival curves — your "don't look back," pre-registered as P-FAN-1.

**The workbench.** One notebook-grade query cookbook (builder deliverable, Tier-E): the parquet
inventory with declared keys, ten worked joins, and the probe-ledger template — so any lane, or
the operator by hand, can interrogate any instant without re-deriving the schema.
