## PART A · THE TRADE, STEP BY STEP

**What you are trading.** A 4-hour momentum system on crypto perpetuals. It buys strength and
sells weakness *late enough to be confirmed and early enough to matter*, risks a fixed unit
behind real structure, presses only when paid, de-risks into trouble, and exits by rule. Charts
needed: the 4h (decisions), the 12h and 1d (walls and tide context). All signals close-confirmed
— nothing intrabar, ever.

**STEP 0 · THE TIDE — may I want this trade?** On the 4h: EMA89 above EMA316 *and* price above
the 316 → longs only. Mirror → shorts only. Neither → stand down. This gate removed 63% of
candidate setups in the last regime and was right to: it keeps you off the wrong side of one-way
tape. *Do not negotiate with the tide.*

**STEP 1 · THE WINDOW — is a setup forming?** The 12 crossing the 89 in your permitted
direction OPENS a window; the opposite cross CLOSES it, unfilled or not. Quality check at the
cross: price must sit at least **0.75 ATR** beyond the 89 (ignition energy — setups born hugging
the line die young). While a window is open you are hunting; when it closes you are done,
no exceptions.

**STEP 2 · THE TRIGGER — enter.** First 12/26 cross inside the open window → **enter at that
bar's close**. Do not wait for bigger confirmation: the "seal" (89/316 turning) arrives ~28
hours late on average — it writes obituaries, not invitations. Missed the trigger? One second
chance only: the first pullback that touches the fast ribbon (12–26 band) where the NEXT 4h
close resumes trend without the counter-cross having fired.

**STEP 3 · THE STOP — where you're wrong.** Behind the nearest real 4h swing pivot within the
last 800 hours, and **never nearer than 1.0 ATR** (the rail). That distance is your R — the unit
every result is measured in. The stop executes; touched means out, adverse-first, no hoping.

**STEP 4 · THE RIDE — hands off until paid.** From entry to **+1R open profit: do nothing.**
No tightening, no trailing, no partial fear-taking. Winners typically dip only ~0.31R against
you before reaching +1R; your stop gives three times that room on purpose.

**STEP 5 · THE TRAIL — arms only after +1R.** Once the trade has been +1R, the ratchet wakes:
every newly *confirmed* 4h swing pivot in your favor (a (2,2) fractal — two bars each side)
drags the stop behind it: pivot ± **0.5 ATR** offset, never nearer than 1.0 ATR from the
confirming close, only ever advancing, and no advance smaller than **0.05 ATR** counts. Before
+1R the trail stays asleep — that sleep is what keeps marginal winners from being shaken into
losers.

**STEP 6 · THE DE-RISK — one planned mercy.** If price comes back and touches the opposing
89/316 band after entry (rallies into it against a short; sells into it against a long): **take
half off at that close.** Expect this to book a small loss — that is its job. Six years of data:
this rule never once banked a gain and still added +5.7R by making the losses it took smaller
than the losses the runner went on to take. It is insurance, not profit-taking. Once per trade.

**STEP 7 · THE WALLS — know where price gets stopped.** The market's firmest ceilings in the
current regime, measured: **EMA 889 on the 12h and the 1d** (≈62% of all approaches rejected on
both clocks, independently), **EMA 3618 on the 4h**, **EMA 4618 on the 1h**. Approaching a
champion wall in your profit direction = the highest-probability zone to pay you *and* to end
the move: tighten attention, expect the de-risk or the exit to earn its keep there. A champion
wall just beyond your stop = structure working for you; a champion wall just beyond your entry
in the profit direction = a short runway — demand more.

**STEP 8 · THE EXITS.** In practice the trail takes you out (it retired the bell almost
entirely: 126 of 127 historical exits). The bell — the counter 12/89 cross, or the 89/316
rolling against you — stays as the backstop for any trade whose trail never armed. One cost
rule: if cumulative funding paid exceeds 1R, close — time is only charged when it literally
bills you.

**THE SHAPE OF THE BUSINESS (so your psychology is pre-loaded):** expect roughly **30% winners**;
losers small (the trail and de-risk average them well under −1R); winners carry the book, and
the average winner survives ~0.31R of adverse before paying. The system's money is made by
*obedience during steps 4–8*, not by cleverness at step 2.
