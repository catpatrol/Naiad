## 1 · Secret Sauce, explained from nothing

Three exponential moving averages — the **9, the 89, the 200** — computed on every timeframe from 5 minutes to 1 day. The 9 is the market's breath, the 89 its stride, the 200 its posture. When price and these lines interact — crossing, testing, rejecting, reclaiming — they emit *events*. The founding intuition: these events **cascade**. A real move begins on the fast frames and climbs the ladder — 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h — each rung confirming the one below. Trade the cascade: enter when the fast frames turn inside a slow-frame trend, add as the ladder confirms, exit when the structure breaks.

The lineage: a Pine scanner over 200 coins → the SS Cascade v11 indicator (governor timeframe sets regime and stage; zones around the slow EMAs mark where business is allowed; PRIME pullback-reclaims pull the trigger; a failure-cross ends the campaign) → a full port into a replay engine, audited on five-plus years of Binance perpetuals under pre-registered hypotheses, a sealed holdout, and published falsifications.

## 2 · What the audit found — the honest arc

Run naively, the machine **lost** (~−1,800R) [ledger]. The forensics then produced a story better than success:

**Entry timing was noise; entry location was real.** Same-instant confluence counting failed everywhere it was tested (D9, P-LAT) [ledger]; the zones worked at 2× null, and the trades the zone gate rejected would have lost almost twice as fast (−1.205 vs −0.577/unit) [ledger].

**The exit was the buried treasure.** A *structural* stop — beyond a real 1h swing pivot — flipped the book to **+565.58 grid / +301.99 strip-best** [ledger]; 52% of trades shaken to its brink converted to ≥+1R [ledger] (Wyckoff's spring, resident in the stop placement all along); and winners paid by **riding to regime break** (opposite-cross exits, 67% winners) [ledger], never by management.

**Costs taxed impatience.** Re-entries carried ~83% of clean-book loss via cost-in-R = fee ÷ stop-width; TC-5 proved the intraday disease is signal quality, not just scale [ledger].

**The add gate froze exactly when adding was wanted.** 97.59% of winning moments offered no pullback-reclaim PRIME [ledger]; yet the 4H cross is the only rung clearing its toll (**1.0711** [1.0534, 1.0903]) [ledger], and the study's single positive cohort was campaigns earning three adds (+4.88, 72.6% win) [ledger].

**Verdict on v11:** a point machine with a governor — superb at *when*, structurally blind to *where* and *how long*. Edge lives in location, exit asymmetry, and the slow frames; losses live in fast-frame impatience and mid-range noise.

## 4 · The research inversion [ratified 2026-08-03]

Operator's directive: *invert — exhaustively scrutinize the big winners to catch more of them; catch more trades that work and fewer that don't. What do winners have in common with winners, and losers with losers?*

The reviewer's old tail-amputation concern does not die; it **operationalizes** as the **Tail-Retention Gauge (TRG)**: every candidate filter, gate, or rule is scored not only on expectancy but on **the fraction of the unfiltered book's top-decile R it retains**. Strip-best asks "is the edge only the tail?"; TRG asks "does your rule keep the tail?" A filter that lifts average expectancy while keeping 40% of the tail is a wealth destroyer in a lab coat. TRG prints beside strip-best from now on, everywhere.

The guard inside the inversion: studying winners alone is selecting on the outcome. **The loser book is the control group.** The object of study is the **discriminant** — for every feature knowable *at or before the decision moment* (curtain-clean), the separation between winner-population prevalence and loser-population prevalence. Promotion bar: five-asset sign replication, held-in-time stability, Benjamini–Hochberg FDR q = 0.10 within each discriminant family. Everything else is scenery.
